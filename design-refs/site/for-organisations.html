What ESOL provision leads tell us

Four chronic problems. Four built in answers.

01 · Capacity Waiting lists run 8 to 14 months.
AI tutor handles the 90% of A1 to B1 work that doesn't need a human. Teachers get routed to the 10% who do. The throughput problem becomes a routing problem.
02 · Underclaim ESFA / ASF funding under claimed.
RARPA Stages 1 to 5 plus an ILR ready CSV plus an ASF GLH split, all generated live from session activity. Month end becomes a download, not a hunt.
03 · Dropout Drop out at A1.
The Bridge Method scaffolds learners from first language to English over six concurrent layers. The instructions are never in a language they don't yet speak.
04 · Chase Compliance team chasing teachers at month end.
Evidence packets auto build per learner, continuously. The compliance team confirms rather than collates.
